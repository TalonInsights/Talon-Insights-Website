The local picture

Telford is well served at the cheap end.

There is no shortage of £30-a-month website providers around Telford and Shropshire. That's a real option, and for some businesses it's the right one. This is for the businesses it isn't right for.

  1. 01

    You want to own it

    Pay-monthly means the site stops existing when you stop paying. Here the domain, the code and the content transfer to you on full payment — you can take it to any other developer, any time.

  2. 02

    You need more than five stock pages

    Custom components, a configurator, a booking flow, a genuine reason a visitor picks you over the firm down the road. Templates run out quickly once a business has something specific to say.

  3. 03

    The site has to bring work in

    A site that looks fine and produces no enquiries is the most common complaint I hear. The words get written before the design starts, and the build is pointed at making the phone ring.

  4. 04

    You want the person, not a queue

    You meet me, I scope it, I build it, I answer the phone afterwards. Nothing is handed to a junior and nothing is sent offshore.
