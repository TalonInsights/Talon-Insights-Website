The Groundwork Visit

I come to you first.

Before anybody talks about price, I come out to your premises and find out how the business actually works. You get a written scope at the end of it, whether or not you go ahead with me.

  1. I come to you

    At your unit, your office, your yard. An hour or two, on site, at a time that suits you. No jargon — I will explain everything in plain English.

Common questions

What exactly is a Groundwork Visit?

A fixed-price visit to your premises. I walk the business with you, work out what is actually needed, and write you a short scope and recommendation. It is £350 to £500 depending on distance and size, and it comes off the price in full if you go ahead with the work.

Do I own the site and the code?

Yes. Your domain, your code, your content, transferred to you on full payment. I keep my own pre-existing frameworks and libraries — that is standard and it is what makes me fast — but the thing built for you is yours, and you can take it to anybody else.

Is it really just you?

Yes. Nothing is handed to a junior and nothing is sent offshore. The person who quotes the job is the person who builds it and the person who picks up the phone afterwards.

What if the scope changes halfway through?

It goes through a written variation with its own price, agreed before I do it. The original number does not move quietly, and you will never get an invoice you were not expecting.

How quickly do you reply?

Within one working day, every time.

I am not technical. Is that a problem?

No, and it is most of my clients. No jargon — I will explain what I am recommending and why, in plain English, and I will tell you when you do not need something.
