Who this suits

Businesses whose reputation is already the selling point.

Shrewsbury runs on independents — shops, studios, trades and professional practices where people buy the person as much as the product. A website's job there isn't to shout. It's to confirm what someone has already been told.

  1. 01

    Independent retail & hospitality

    Somewhere that looks as considered as the shop does, loads instantly on a phone, and makes opening hours, location and booking obvious in one glance.

  2. 02

    Trades and makers

    Real photographs of finished work, plainly priced or plainly explained, and structured so Google can tell exactly what you do and where you do it.

  3. 03

    Professional practices

    Solicitors, accountants, consultants — where credibility is the entire product and a dated website quietly undermines it every day.

  4. 04

    Period and listed premises

    If your building is part of the story, the site should show it properly rather than hiding it behind stock photography.
