# Fonts

Inter and Space Grotesk, the two families the site loads at runtime, kept here
as TTFs so `ogcards.py` can render the Open Graph cards in the real brand faces
rather than a system substitute.

Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, which permits
redistribution. Source: Google Fonts.

- Inter - https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter
- Space Grotesk - https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Space+Grotesk

These are also the files needed to self-host the fonts and drop the two
external Google origins from the CSP, which is a separate outstanding job.
