Common questions

Asked before, answered straight.

Why a three-month minimum?

Because SEO genuinely takes three to six months to show results, and a one-month trial proves nothing except that you spent a month's fee. Three months is the shortest honest test. After that it's monthly rolling — if it isn't earning its keep, you stop.

Can you guarantee rankings?

No, and nobody honest can — Google's results aren't for sale to consultants. What I guarantee is the work: the deliverables listed in your tier, done every month, reported honestly. That's the part anyone can actually control.

What if my website is the problem?

Then I'll tell you before taking a retainer. SEO on a slow, broken site is pouring water into a cracked bucket — sometimes the right first step is a rebuild, and you'd rather hear that up front than three months in.

Do I keep the work if I leave?

Yes. The content, the citations, the profile improvements, the rankings — they're attached to your business, not to me. No hostages.
