Terms.

The short version of how engagements work. Every project also gets its own written agreement.

Scope and price

  • Every project starts from a written scope with a fixed price and milestone dates.
  • Changes are agreed through a written variation, priced before the work happens.
  • Payment is staged: deposit, milestones, completion.

Ownership

  • On full payment, the project's code, content and domain belong to the client.
  • I retain my pre-existing tools, frameworks and libraries used to build it.
  • I retain the right to show the work in my portfolio unless we agree otherwise in writing.

After launch

  • Every build includes a warranty period for bugs, stated in the project agreement.
  • Ongoing support, hosting management or SEO are separate, clearly priced arrangements — never a surprise invoice.

The formal bit

Each engagement is governed by a signed agreement covering scope, payment, intellectual property, confidentiality, data protection, liability and termination. You'll see it before any work starts, and it's written to be read.
