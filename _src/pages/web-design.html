Common questions

Asked before, answered straight.

Which areas do you cover?

Telford and Shropshire, Wolverhampton, Birmingham and the wider West Midlands. There are dedicated pages for web design in Telford and web design in Shrewsbury.

How long does a website take?

A starter site is typically two to three weeks from agreed scope; a business site four to six. You get real dates in the written scope, and I deliver against them.

Do you write the content?

Yes — drafted by me, corrected by you. You know your trade; I know how to write for buyers and for search. It goes live when you're happy with every word.

What about hosting and the domain?

Both live in accounts in your name. Hosting for a site like this is typically free or a few pounds a month. No monthly fee to me to keep your own website online.

Can you rebuild what we already have?

Usually, and you keep what works — the content that ranks, the photos that are good. The Groundwork Visit establishes what's worth keeping before anything is thrown away.

Will it show up on Google?

The build gives Google everything it asks for — speed, structure, schema, mobile. Ranking against competitors over time is a separate, ongoing discipline; that's the local SEO service, and I'll tell you honestly whether you need it.
