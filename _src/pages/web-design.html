How it's decided

Nothing on the page is a taste decision.

Colour, type size, page order, the words themselves — each traces back to something learned about your buyers before design starts. Most of it is learned at the Groundwork Visit, standing where your customers stand.

demographics

Your buyers skew 55+, and two-thirds arrive on a phone

Larger base type, stronger contrast, bigger tap targets — and one idea per screen instead of four

groundwork visit

Most work arrives by recommendation, not search

The first screen confirms the recommendation — face, credentials, proof — rather than selling to a stranger

customer language

On the phone, customers say "damp coming through", never "penetrating moisture ingress"

Headings written in the customer's words, so the page reads like the call did — and ranks for what people actually type

competitor scan

Every rival within ten miles is blue, corporate and interchangeable

A colour scheme chosen to be owned locally — distinct in a Google results page, consistent with your vans and signage

objections

The hesitation you hear most is "how much mess, and how long?"

That answer goes above the fold with photographs — not buried in an FAQ where only the committed find it

Common questions

Asked before, answered straight.

Which areas do you cover?

Telford and Shropshire, Wolverhampton, Birmingham and the wider West Midlands. There are dedicated pages for web design in Telford and web design in Shrewsbury.

How long does a website take?

A starter site is typically two to three weeks from agreed scope; a business site four to six. You get real dates in the written scope, and I deliver against them.

Do you write the content?

Yes — drafted by me, corrected by you. You know your trade; I know how to write for buyers and for search. It goes live when you're happy with every word.

What about hosting and the domain?

Both live in accounts in your name. Hosting for a site like this is typically free or a few pounds a month. No monthly fee to me to keep your own website online.

Can you rebuild what we already have?

Usually, and you keep what works — the content that ranks, the photos that are good. The Groundwork Visit establishes what's worth keeping before anything is thrown away.

Will it show up on Google?

The build gives Google everything it asks for — speed, structure, schema, mobile. Ranking against competitors over time is a separate, ongoing discipline; that's the local SEO service, and I'll tell you honestly whether you need it.
