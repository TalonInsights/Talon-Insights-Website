Privacy policy.

Plain English, as short as the law allows.

Last updated: August 2026.

Talon Insights ("I", "me") is a sole trader based in Telford, Shropshire, building websites and software for businesses. This policy explains what personal data I collect through this website and how I handle it, in line with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What I collect

  • Enquiry details. If you use the contact form or email me: your name, business name, contact details, and whatever you write in your message. Nothing on the form is required beyond a way of replying to you.
  • Basic analytics. Aggregate visit statistics through Vercel Web Analytics — pages viewed, approximate location by country, device type and referring site. It sets no cookies and does not follow you between websites, so there is nothing here to consent to. No advertising trackers, and no selling of data.

Why, and on what basis

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss work — legitimate interest.
  • To deliver contracted work — performance of a contract.
  • To keep business records — legal obligation.

What I don't do

  • No selling or renting of your data. Ever.
  • No marketing lists — you won't get a newsletter you didn't ask for.
  • No sharing with third parties beyond the service providers that make the site and the contact form run, each bound by their own data-processing terms. Those are Vercel, which hosts the site and provides the analytics, and Formspree, which delivers form submissions to my inbox. Both are US companies, so an enquiry you send does leave the UK on its way to me. Nobody else sees it.

Retention

Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept for 6 years, as required for tax purposes.

Your rights

You can ask what I hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to my holding it at all. Email taloninsights@gmail.com and I'll respond within one month. If you're unhappy with how I handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
