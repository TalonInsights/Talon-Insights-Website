The rules

How the numbers behave.

  • Fixed means fixed. The price in the written scope is the price on the invoice.
  • Changes are written down. New scope goes through a written variation with its own price, agreed before the work happens — never an invoice surprise.
  • Payment is staged. Deposit, milestones, completion. You always know where you are.
  • Ownership transfers on full payment. Domain, code, content — yours.
