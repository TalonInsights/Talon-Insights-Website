Published, so you can rule me out without a phone call.

Hiding prices reads as "expensive" or evasive, so mine are here. Websites and software are always fixed price. Anything bespoke is quoted after a Groundwork Visit — because quoting bespoke work off a phone call is guesswork, and guesswork becomes your problem later.

Founding projects

I'm building a case study in each service line.

Four services, and I want a properly documented piece of work behind every one of them — real figures, real screenshots, the client's own words. Until each has that, I'm taking a small number of projects at a founding rate.

The trade is straightforward. You get the same scope, the same written quote and the same standard of work for less. I get something I can show the next person who asks what I've done. The reduction depends on the job and on how much of it I can publish, and it's agreed in writing with everything else before anything starts.

It isn't a discount for the sake of one, and it isn't permanent. Once a service line has its case study, the published prices below are the prices.

What I ask in return

  • Permission to write the work up and name your business.
  • Screenshots or photographs of the finished thing.
  • A measurable result, once there is one worth measuring.
  • A few sentences in your own words — after you've seen it working, not before.

If you'd rather stay anonymous, that's fine — it just means standard rates.

The rules

How the numbers behave.

  • Fixed means fixed. The price in the written scope is the price on the invoice.

  • Payment is staged. Deposit, milestones, completion. You always know where you are.
  • Ownership transfers on full payment. Domain, code, content — yours.
