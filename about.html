I'm Talon Herring.

I build software and websites for businesses around Telford and the West Midlands, and I run every job like a proper project — because running projects is what I do for a living.

Both halves of the job.

Most freelance developers can't run a project to time and budget. Most project managers can't build. The whole point of Talon Insights is that you get both in one person — and that person is the one you met on day one.

Talon Insights is my full-time work. Behind it sit four years running projects in environments where scope, evidence and deadlines are not negotiable, and a Level 4 Associate Project Manager qualification. Alongside that I've been writing software since school: today that means Supabase backends, Three.js, Firebase and Stripe, shipped through a GitHub and Vercel pipeline. I'm also completing a BSc in Computer Science alongside client work.

Before this I built and ran Talon Detailing to full booking capacity — including its own multi-step booking and payment system with an admin dashboard — and then closed it deliberately when the commercial case changed. I build businesses, not just websites, and I know when a decision is the right one.

It's just me. That's the point. Nothing is handed to a junior, nothing goes offshore, and when you ring, the person who answers is the person who built it.
