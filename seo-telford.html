Where Telford is won

The map pack, then the page.

Somebody searching "electrician Telford" on a phone sees three map results before anything else. Getting into that trio is a different job from ranking a web page, and it's the cheaper of the two to win.

  1. 01

    Google Business Profile first

    The single biggest local-pack lever and the least expensive: correct primary category, full service list, real photographs, weekly posts, and a system for asking every happy customer for a review.

  2. 02

    Consistent name, address, phone

    Across Yell, Bing Places, FreeIndex, the Shropshire Chamber and the directories that actually carry weight. Inconsistency here quietly suppresses local rankings.

  3. 03

    Pages that match Telford intent

    What people here actually type — trade plus town — rather than the vanity phrases that look impressive in a report and are searched by nobody.

  4. 04

    The technical floor

    Speed, mobile, crawlability, LocalBusiness schema. Most local competitors have never touched any of it, which is precisely why it's worth doing.

  5. 05

    Reporting you can audit

    Rankings, calls, direction requests, enquiries — monthly, in plain English. If it isn't working, the report says so rather than burying it.

Straight answers

Why "guaranteed page one" is always a lie.

Nobody controls Google's results. What a supplier controls is the work — which is why every tier here is a list of named deliverables rather than a vague monthly retainer, and why the report exists to prove they happened.

  • Three-month minimum, then monthly rolling. Long enough to be a fair test, short enough that you're never trapped.
  • You keep everything if you leave. Content, citations, profile improvements and rankings attach to your business, not to me.
  • If your site is the bottleneck, I'll say so before taking a retainer. SEO on a broken site is water into a cracked bucket.

Common questions

Telford, specifically.

How much does SEO cost in Telford?

Three tiers depending on how contested your terms are, on a three-month minimum then monthly rolling, with every deliverable listed so you can check you are getting it. Quoted after a free call. Founding rates are available while I build case studies across every service line.

Can you guarantee first page?

No — and treat anyone who does with suspicion. Google's rankings aren't for sale to consultants. I guarantee the work in your tier, done monthly and evidenced.

How long before we see anything?

Three to six months for meaningful movement. Google Business Profile work often shows sooner, sometimes in weeks — which is exactly why it comes first.

Do we need a new website first?

Sometimes. If the site is slow, unusable on a phone or structurally broken, SEO spend is wasted until it's fixed — and I'd rather lose a retainer than take one that can't work. If a rebuild is the honest first step, I'll tell you.
