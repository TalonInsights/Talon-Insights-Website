Where Telford is won

The map pack, then the page.

Somebody searching "electrician Telford" on a phone sees three map results before anything else. Getting into that trio is a different job from ranking a web page, and it's the cheaper of the two to win.

  1. 01

    Google Business Profile first

    The single biggest local-pack lever and the least expensive: correct primary category, full service list, real photographs, weekly posts, and a system for asking every happy customer for a review.

  2. 02

    Consistent name, address, phone

    Across Yell, Bing Places, FreeIndex, the Shropshire Chamber and the directories that actually carry weight. Inconsistency here quietly suppresses local rankings.

  3. 03

    Pages that match Telford intent

    What people here actually type — trade plus town — rather than the vanity phrases that look impressive in a report and are searched by nobody.

  4. 04

    The technical floor

    Speed, mobile, crawlability, LocalBusiness schema. Most local competitors have never touched any of it, which is precisely why it's worth doing.

  5. 05

    Reporting you can audit

    Rankings, calls, direction requests, enquiries — monthly, in plain English. If it isn't working, the report says so rather than burying it.
